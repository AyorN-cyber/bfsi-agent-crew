# Loan-to-value policy

## Loan to value ratio limit
Loan-to-value (LTV) is the loan amount divided by the appraised property value. An LTV of 80% or lower passes. An LTV above 80% and up to 95% requires a larger down payment review or mortgage insurance and is referred to a human underwriter. An LTV above 95% is declined.

## Appraisal
Property value is the lower of the purchase price and the appraised value.
