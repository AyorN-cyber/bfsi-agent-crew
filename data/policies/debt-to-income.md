# Debt-to-income policy

## Debt to income ratio limit
Debt-to-income (DTI) is total monthly obligations, including the proposed loan payment, divided by gross monthly income. A DTI of 43% or lower passes. A DTI above 43% and up to 50% is referred to a human underwriter. A DTI above 50% is declined.

## Income verification
Salaried income is verified with two recent pay statements. Self-employed income uses a two-year average from tax returns.
