# Fair lending and human review

## Human review
Automated output is a recommendation only. Every decline and every referral is confirmed by a human underwriter before anything is communicated to the applicant. Reviewers must record the reasons for the final decision.

## Adverse action reasons
When an application is declined, the applicant receives a notice that names the specific reasons. Decisions rely only on documented criteria in this policy library, never on protected characteristics such as race, religion, sex, age or national origin.
